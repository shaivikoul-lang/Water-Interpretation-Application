Community water data viewer

Understand Highlands Ranch water-quality data

  • What is this?

    A community tool that turns Colorado’s public monitoring data for Highlands Ranch Water into plain-language charts and short explanations—not a lab test of water at your tap.

  • Who is it for?

    Highlands Ranch residents who want context on reported water quality, trends over time, and links to official guidance from regulators and your utility.

  • How to use it

    Pick Dashboard view for charts and common topics (PFAS, lead, hardness, taste & odor), or Classic view for simple year-by-year tables.

About PFAS

People often first hear about PFAS through drinking water. PFAS is a larger group of persistent chemicals connected to environmental chemistry, industry, and cleanup research—not only the tap. This tool focuses on Highlands Ranch public monitoring data and explains what’s reported in plain language. It is not a test of water at your faucet.

Your water is monitored to keep it safe. Highlands Ranch Water regularly tests its supply and works to meet state and federal drinking water standards. This tool simply lets you explore that same public data for yourself.
